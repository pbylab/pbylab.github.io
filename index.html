About the Lab

Where Geospatial Data
Meets Deep Intelligence

PBYAIL is a research group at Chulalongkorn University dedicated to advancing AI methods for Earth observation, cadastral systems, and geospatial intelligence. We build systems that are autonomy-driven, interpretable, and designed for real-world impact at national scale.

🛰️
Earth Observation
Satellite & aerial imagery analysis for spatial decision-making
🧠
Deep Learning
Novel architectures for vision and segmentation tasks
🗺️
Geo-AI
AI-powered land administration & cadastral digitization
⚖️
Responsible AI
Ethical, interpretable systems with societal benefit

Research Areas

Our Focus Domains

01
🛰️
AI for Earth Observation
Developing deep learning systems for satellite and aerial image interpretation at national scale, enabling precise land-use mapping and environmental monitoring.
02
🔀
Multimodal Machine Learning
Fusing heterogeneous data sources — imagery, text, geospatial signals — into unified representations that power richer environmental understanding.
03
🌊
Diffusion Models
Applying generative diffusion models to remote sensing synthesis, data augmentation, and uncertainty-guided segmentation for high-stakes applications.
04
📍
Geospatial Intelligence
Converting raw geographic data into actionable intelligence for land administration, urban planning, and infrastructure decision-making.
05
⚖️
Responsible & Ethical AI
Ensuring AI systems deployed in government and civic contexts are fair, transparent, and aligned with human values — AI for Social Good.
06
🤝
Human–AI Interaction
Building interfaces and models that enable natural, intuitive collaboration between humans and intelligent systems in professional workflows.
